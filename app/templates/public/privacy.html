Data Boundary & Ethics

Privacy Boundary & Data Collection Policy

A plain-language, transparent commitment detailing what we build, what we never collect, and how to protect yourself.

Our Foundational Privacy Commitment

CaseBridge does not intentionally require, collect, or persist direct identity information for anonymous submissions. Our database schemas and server logic are architected to eliminate unnecessary identity collection from the ground up.

When you submit a grievance, your report is identified solely by an unpredictable, high-entropy tracking key. The system records your narrative and chosen category—not your personal dossier.

Information We Never Collect or Store in Complaint Records

❌ No Names or Student IDs

The intake form contains no fields for your full name, matriculation ID, social security, or passport numbers.

❌ No Email or Phone Numbers

We do not ask for or store contact details. Communication takes place strictly through the anonymous tracking portal.

❌ No Client IP Address Logging

The database schema omits client IP fields, and server logging filters strip network identifiers before persisting log lines.

❌ No Third-Party Trackers or Ad Pixels

Zero third-party trackers, analytics scripts, social widgets, or advertising telemetry are loaded on any CaseBridge page.

⚠ A Candid Note on Network Reality & Anonymity Limits

No responsible engineering team can claim "100% absolute untraceability" across the internet. While CaseBridge does not store your identity, internet service providers (ISPs), campus Wi-Fi access points, and upstream network proxies maintain routing logs outside the software’s perimeter.

If you are in a high-threat situation where campus network traffic might be monitored by an adversarial actor, we strongly recommend accessing CaseBridge using a personal mobile hotspot, an off-campus public Wi-Fi network, or the Tor Browser.

How to Preserve Your Anonymity in Narrative Details

  • Avoid Unique Identifying Facts: Be careful not to describe facts that only one person could know (e.g. "as the only international student in the 8:00 AM laboratory section").
  • Sanitize Screenshots & Attachments: When uploading PDF or image files, inspect them to ensure your personal email address, institutional login username, or phone number does not appear in window titles or document headers.
  • Keep Your Tracking Code Secure: Do not share your tracking code with others. Anyone with access to that code can view committee responses and message history.
