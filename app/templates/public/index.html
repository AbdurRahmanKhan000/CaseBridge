How We Protect Your Identity

The system does not intentionally require direct identity information for anonymous submissions. We do not ask for your name, student registration number, personal phone, or email address. We also actively avoid persisting client IP addresses in complaint records.

Note on network reality: While our software does not store identity details, internet service providers and campus Wi-Fi networks log transport packets. If your threat model requires network-level detachment, access this site over a private mobile connection or Tor. Learn more on our Privacy Boundary page.

How CaseBridge Works

A clear, predictable four-stage journey from intake to independent committee resolution.

1

Submit Anonymously

Describe what occurred factually under one of the 8 approved institutional categories. Attach supporting evidence if safe.

2

Save Your Tracking Code

Receive a high-entropy 16-character code (CB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). Store it carefully—it is your only key to return.

3

Two-Way Dialogue

The independent committee reviews narratives and can post clarifying questions. Reply anonymously at any time.

4

Resolution & Audit

Track workflow milestones from In Review to Resolved. Every transition is recorded in an immutable technical audit trail.
