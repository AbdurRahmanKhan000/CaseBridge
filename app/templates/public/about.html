About CaseBridge & The ARK Ecosystem

Bridging student safety and institutional accountability through independent, privacy-first grievance management.

Our Institutional Mission

Universities and academic communities thrive when students, staff, and faculty can voice concerns regarding unethical behavior, harassment, discrimination, or administrative misconduct without fear of academic retaliation or career detriment.

Traditional institutional reporting mechanisms frequently require immediate identity disclosure as a prerequisite for intake. For many vulnerable individuals, this creates a chilling barrier that leaves serious issues unaddressed.

CaseBridge serves as an institutional bridge: providing complainants verified software-level privacy, while giving university ethics committees the workflow, evidence management, and communication tools needed to conduct thorough, equitable inquiries.

Committee Composition & Independence

To preserve impartiality, grievance reviews are handled by an independent Ethics & Case Review Committee comprising designated faculty ombudspersons, student welfare officers, and compliance administrators.

  • Conflict-of-Interest Controls: Cases involving specific academic departments are routed away from staff belonging to those departments.
  • Immutable Event Logs: All status transitions, assignments, and escalations are recorded in an append-only log that cannot be altered or purged.
  • Controlled Escalations: If a case remains unattended beyond its resolution SLA, automatic escalation alerts are generated.
