Process & Transparency

How the CaseBridge Process Works

A clear, predictable, and retaliation-free pathway from intake to resolution findings.

1

1. Submit Your Case Anonymously

Choose the appropriate institutional grievance category (e.g. Harassment, Bullying, Academic Issue, Discrimination, or Unfair Treatment). State the facts objectively. You do not provide your name, student ID, or personal contact info.

Zero Identity Required
2

2. Receive and Store Your Private Tracking Code

Upon submission, the system generates an unpredictable 16-character tracking code (CB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). Save this code immediately.

Security Note: CaseBridge stores only an irreversible SHA-256 peppered hash of your code. Because we do not store your email or student record, lost codes cannot be retrieved or reset by staff.
3

3. Independent Committee Review

The complaint enters the intake queue with the initial status of Received. The Ethics & Case Review Committee verifies category classification and assesses urgency against institutional Service Level Agreements (SLAs).

4

4. Case Assignment & Conflict Checking

The case moves to Assigned. An authorized investigator or ombudsperson is designated to handle the matter. If a case touches on a specific department, staff from that department are excluded to prevent conflicts of interest.

5

5. Secure Anonymous Messaging

Investigators can post clarifying inquiries directly to your tracking thread. You can answer and submit additional details at any time using your tracking code without creating an account or revealing your identity.

6

6. Progress Updates & Milestones

As investigations progress, status milestones reflect current proceedings: In Review or Action Required. If an investigation takes longer than the assigned SLA, an automated overdue flag ensures immediate escalation.

7

7. Formal Resolution and Closure

Once findings, corrective measures, or policy remedies are decided, the committee updates the case to Resolved or Closed. Formal outcome summaries are posted to your tracking page for your records.

Institutional Response SLA Benchmarks

Every case is bounded by resolution deadlines to guarantee timely oversight.

Within 24 Hours

High Priority
